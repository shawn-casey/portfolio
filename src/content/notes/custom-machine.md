**Machine Shop Assistant**
Woburn, MA · June to August 2022

The summer before my freshman year of college. Not my father's company. He has spent his career in CNC machining elsewhere, which is how I grew up around machine tools.

## The work

The shop had a badly disorganized storage room and I was the person sent to fix it. I cleared it out, installed and labeled heavy-duty shelving, relocated fixtures, vise jaws, and workholding, moved the smaller machines with pallet jacks, and identified unmarked tooling by measuring it with micrometers against spec.

Then I catalogued every part number and built a **Python and SQL inventory system covering 1,000+ CNC fixtures**, with an interface for locating parts by category. A manual hunt through the shop became a lookup.

## Why it's on this site

I was the labor standing in the messy storage room, and I ended up writing software to fix the problem I was physically inside. That's the same instinct behind [[Marcel]], four years later.
