Two things in different domains off the same instinct: find a specific person with a specific expensive problem, then go solve it.

- [[Mobile Detailing Business]] · learned the trade at a dealership, bought equipment, sold to students, turned half of it into a fundraiser.
- [[Marcel]] · a CNC diagnostic agent. Same move, aimed at machine shops instead of parking lots.

Detailing cars taught me three things a CS degree does not. **Pricing is positioning**, not math; charge too little and you've told people what you think the work is worth. **Throughput is the business**; doubling revenue meant a second pair of hands, not a higher price. And **distribution beats product**; the [[Mobile Detailing Business|HuskyTHON partnership]] grew the customer base more than being good at detailing did.

Two years around an early-stage hardware company ([[Cytrence Technologies]]) filled in the rest: what it looks like when the constraint isn't the technology.
