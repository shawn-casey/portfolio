The favorite is a section of the **Tour du Mont Blanc**, the circuit through France, Italy, and Switzerland around the massif.

What makes it different from most American hiking is that it's a hike *through* a place rather than away from one. You cross cols in the morning and come down into a valley where people live, eat somewhere, and go up the other side. The huts are staffed. Mountain travel as ordinary infrastructure instead of as an expedition, and it reframed what I think a trail is for.

Domestically most of it runs through the [[National Parks]] list. Same legs as [[Mountain Biking]], much slower.
