Started in middle school, raced through high school. Oldest thing on this list and the one that set the pattern for everything else in [[Hobbies]].

Favorite trails: **Kingdom Trails**, East Burke, Vermont. A hundred-plus miles of singletrack stitched across private land, held together by landowners who agreed to let it happen.

Racing taught me the thing I keep re-learning elsewhere: **you go where you look.** Fixate on the rock you're trying to miss and you will hit it. The skill isn't avoiding the obstacle, it's keeping your eyes on the line past it.
