**16 of 63.** An ongoing project with no deadline.

Favorite so far is **Big Bend**, in far west Texas where the Rio Grande turns north. Least crowded major park in the lower 48, because it's genuinely hard to get to: the nearest real airport is hours away and there's nothing in between.

What you get for the drive is desert, a mountain range in the middle of it, a river canyon at the bottom, and one of the darkest skies in the country. The Chisos are a sky island, an isolated forested range holding species stranded there since the last time the climate was wetter. You climb out of desert into pines in a couple of hours.

Most of these are [[Hiking]] trips. A few have been [[Mountain Biking]] trips.
