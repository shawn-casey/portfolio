**B.S. Computer Science, Minor in Mathematics**
Storrs, CT · GPA 3.73 · graduated May 2026

## Teaching

**TA for Quantum Computing and Introduction to Computer Science.**

Two different jobs. Intro CS is the first wall: students convinced they aren't "a programming person" because a semicolon ruined their afternoon. Almost all of that is confidence and debugging technique, not aptitude.

Quantum is the opposite failure mode. Strong students whose intuition works against them, arriving with a picture of superposition from popular science that's wrong in a specific way and produces confident incorrect answers about measurement. Teaching it is mostly demolition before construction.

Doing that while writing [[Quantum Public Key Encryption for NISQ Devices]] was the useful part.

## Philanthropy Chairman, Pi Kappa Phi

**$55,000+ raised** for The Ability Experience, Pi Kappa Phi's national philanthropy supporting people with disabilities. Chairman is an operations job wearing a title: running events a hundred-plus people have to show up to, and building something repeatable enough to survive the person who ran it graduating.

## DEI Captain, HuskyTHON

![|wide](/img/huskython.webp)

HuskyTHON is UConn's dance marathon for Connecticut Children's Hospital: a year of fundraising ending in an all-night event with thousands of students. As DEI Captain my job was reach, making sure it drew from the whole campus rather than the organizations that always show up. Dance marathons recruit through existing social networks, which quietly narrows the pool every year.

> [!success] HuskyTHON raised $2.1M organization-wide for Connecticut Children's.

> [!info] Two numbers, kept separate
> The $2.1M is the organization's total. Separately I personally raised **$3,000** for the same hospital by donating half the earnings of my [[Mobile Detailing Business]] through a HuskyTHON partnership, a different role at the same event.

I also did telemetry work for the Formula SAE team here: [[Tie Rod Force Anomaly Detection]]. [[Belcan]] ran part-time through my last semester.
