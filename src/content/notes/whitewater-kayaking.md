Newest thing I've picked up, on the **Chattooga River**: federally designated Wild and Scenic, which in practice means no development on the banks and no road out if it goes wrong.

Being a beginner at something with real consequences, after being good at [[Mountain Biking]] for a decade, is humbling and I recommend it. On a bike I know what my body will do. In a boat the river is still doing most of the deciding.

The mental model is different in a way I like. On a trail the terrain is fixed and you pick a line through it. On a river the terrain is moving, and the line you picked from the eddy stops existing a second after you commit to it.
